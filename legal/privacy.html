Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 October 2026 ·日本語

Kanjimo is run by Shanewas Ahmed Nabil, an individual in Japan ("we"). This policy covers the website and study app at kanjimo.shanewas.com. Contact: support@shanewas.com.

What we collect and why

Email addressTo sign you in with one-time codes and to send notices about your account. We never send marketing mail unless you opt in.
Google sign-inIf you choose "Continue with Google", Google sends us your email address and confirms that Google has verified it. We ask for nothing else (no name, photo or contacts) and keep no Google token after sign-in.
Study progressYour review history, settings and streak, stored on our server so they sync between your devices.
IP address and browser nameKept with each sign-in session and in server logs, to keep accounts secure and to limit abuse.

Kanjimo is free, so we take no payments and hold no payment details. We use no analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

Cookies and browser storage

One strictly necessary cookie keeps you signed in. The app also keeps your progress, its sync state and your light or dark theme in your browser's local storage. None of these are used for tracking, so we show no consent banner.

Who processes data for us

CloudflareNetwork delivery and security. United States, with servers worldwide.
ContaboServer hosting. Germany.
ResendDelivery of sign-in and account mail. United States.
GoogleSign-in with a Google account, only if you choose it. United States.

Germany is covered by the EU General Data Protection Regulation, which Japan's Personal Information Protection Commission recognises as equivalent. The United States has no comprehensive federal privacy law; each provider above is bound by contract to protect the data and to use it only to provide its service to us.

How long we keep it

  • Account and study progress: until you delete your account.
  • Sign-in sessions: until they expire, at most one year.
  • Sign-in codes: one day.
  • Server logs: up to 90 days.

Your rights

You can see, correct, stop the use of, or delete your data. Delete your account at any time from the Account screen; this removes your email, progress and sessions. For anything else, write to support@shanewas.com and we will answer without delay. If you live in the EU or UK, you have the same rights under the GDPR, including data portability and the right to complain to your supervisory authority.

Security

All traffic is encrypted with TLS. Sign-in codes and session tokens are stored only as hashes. The service runs with the least privileges it needs, and backups are kept on access-controlled storage.

Changes

We will email you before any material change to this policy takes effect.
